## Full description

**Business Ledger** is a downloadable books app for people who run more than one small business or side hustle.

Open it in Chrome. Add your businesses. Track income and expenses, invoices, quotes, checklists and profit — with South African tax-year summaries (1 March – 28/29 February). Your data stays in your browser. Export JSON backups whenever you want.

### You get
- Full app ZIP (open `index.html` — no install circus)  
- Setup wizard (workspace, ZAR default, VAT yes/no, businesses)  
- Optional Listings tab (off by default if you do not sell online)  
- README for buyers  
- 12 months of free updates via email  

### You do not get
- A monthly subscription  
- Cloud login / multi-user access  
- Bank feeds  
- Personal tax advice  

### Requirements
Modern desktop Chrome or Edge. Export backups regularly (especially before clearing browser data).

### Refund policy
14-day money-back guarantee. If Business Ledger isn't right for you, email within 14 days of purchase (the address on your receipt) for a full refund.

## FAQ (store fields)
**How do I open it?** Unzip → open `index.html` in Chrome. Details in README.  
**Is my data private?** Yes — local browser storage + your own backup files.  
**VAT?** You choose VAT registered yes/no in setup. The app does not file at SARS for you.  
**Updates?** Email us from your purchase address; we send update ZIPs for 12 months.
