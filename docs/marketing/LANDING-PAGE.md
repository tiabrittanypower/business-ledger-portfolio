## FAQ
**Do I need an internet connection?**  
Only to download. Day-to-day use is local.

**Where is my data stored?**  
In your browser on this computer. Export JSON backups and keep them with your records.

**Is this tax advice?**  
No. Tax year views are a preparation helper. Use your documents and a practitioner / SARS guidance when filing.

**Can I hide the Listings tab?**  
Yes — leave it off in setup, or change it anytime in Settings.

**Mac or Windows?**  
Any OS with a modern Chrome/Edge browser.

**Updates?**  
Founders/Standard include 12 months of free update ZIPs via the email you used to buy.
