# Business Ledger — go-to-market plan

**Product:** One-time downloadable multi-business books app (static HTML/JS, local data).  
**Primary market:** South African freelancers and multi-side-hustle owners.  
**Sales channel (v1):** Gumroad or Payhip.  
**Repo / PR:** https://github.com/tiabrittanypower/business-ledger/pull/1

---

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Books for people with more than one small business — on your computer, built for the SA tax year.

**Buyer:** Freelancers, creators, bakers, consultants, and side-hustlers with 2–5 trades who do not want a monthly accounting subscription.

**Why not Wave/Xero:** One-time price · data stays local · multi-business in one place · SA tax year (1 Mar–28/29 Feb) · optional Listings only if they sell online.

**Not for:** Large VAT-heavy firms, multi-user teams, people who need live bank feeds or formal SARS eFiling.

---

## Pricing (ZAR)

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Founders (first 50) | R149–R199 | Full app ZIP + 12 months of free updates via email |
| Standard | R249–R349 | Same |
| Later add-on | +R99 | 30-min setup call or invoice template pack |

**Refund:** 14 days if it will not run on their machine (builds trust).

**Delivery:** ZIP (`index.html`, assets, README, LICENSE). No licence server for v1.

## 6-week launch

### Week 0 — Prep
- [ ] Merge product PR / freeze buyer build
- [ ] Publish landing page (copy in `docs/marketing/LANDING-PAGE.md`)
- [ ] Create Gumroad/Payhip product (copy in `docs/marketing/GUMROAD-LISTING.md`)
- [ ] Record 30–45s walkthrough + 5 screenshots
- [ ] FAQ live on landing + store page

### Week 1 — Soft launch
- [ ] Share with warm network (clients, WhatsApp, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Founders pricing for first 20–50
- [ ] Collect 1 testimonial per early buyer

### Weeks 2–3 — Content
2–3 posts/week: multi-business books without a monthly fee · SA tax year prep · export backup demo · optional Listings tab.

### Weeks 4–5 — Communities
Helpful posts in SA freelancer / SMME / side-hustle groups; link in bio or first comment — no spam dumps.

### Week 6 — Paid test
R500–R1 000 Meta ads to landing; keep only ads that drive checkout clicks.

**Soft-launch goal:** 10 paid buyers in 2 weeks.
