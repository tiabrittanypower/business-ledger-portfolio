Corporate Identity Manual02

Introduction & brand story

A private books workspace, not a public brand site

Business Ledger is a private multi-business accounting workspace to keep the books for side businesses: income, expenses, invoices, P&L reports and South African tax-year summaries. Source · index.html meta + README

Purpose of this manual

This is the identity system for the Ledger product chrome (sidebar mark, blue actions, invoice print). It is not a mockup board and not a wireframe. The product itself is a private finance tool — do not publish this manual or the app.

Brand essence

Private books. Five trades. One screen. Draft — confirm

Keep the books for every side business in one place. Source · dashboard hero

Product title: Business Ledger – Multi-Business Books. Legal trading name is not in project docs. Draft — confirm

This product is

A local, static HTML/CSS/JS books tool with browser localStorage, ZAR formatting (en-ZA), South Africa tax year 1 Mar–28/29 Feb, and a switcher for five side businesses. No VAT is added because the businesses are marked as not VAT-registered. Source · project.md, app.js, README

This product is not

A public website, a bank, a SARS eFiling product, or a multi-tenant SaaS. It is also not a substitute for a tax practitioner. Backups live as JSON in the project backups/ folder. Source · README / project.md

Umbrella

Ledger tracks multiple businesses side by side. Each business has its own colour chip in the UI. Ledger blue (#1f5fbf) is the product identity — not a replacement for those product brands. Source · project.md, app.js businesses[]

Color palette · usage, contrast, themes

When to use which colour

RoleUse forDo not use for
Primary blueMark, active nav, primary buttons, invoice rule, theme-color, WT tabBakery or notebook rows (those keep their business chips)
TealNSR live KPIs, positive status pills, secondary emphasisReplacing the BL mark
GoldMonth-alert action, Example Bakery chip, warm notices (#fff8e8 / #f0d59f)Body copy (contrast fails WCAG AA for 14px)
CoralDestructive icon buttons, blocked status, Example Notebooks chipPrimary actions
Ink / paper / softAll long text; page ground is soft, panels are paperAccent fills

Contrast (sRGB, relative luminance)

Ink on paper ≈ 16.5:1 AAA. Primary on white ≈ 6.1:1 AA for body. White on primary ≈ 6.1:1 AA. Teal on white ≈ 5.5:1 AA. Gold on white ≈ 3.2:1 — large text only (18px+ / 14px bold). Coral on white ≈ 3.9:1 — large text / UI chrome, not body. Muted on paper is for captions, not legal copy.

Corporate Identity Manual10

Do’s and don’ts

Quick reference

Do

  • Use the 8px rounded-square BL mark in primary blue.
  • Keep “Private books” as the only sidebar tagline.
  • Set body text in ink on paper or soft.
  • Use primary for active nav and the sole primary CTA on a form.
  • Colour checklist rows with the business chip, not the BL mark.
  • Print invoices with the 3px primary rule and “No VAT”.
  • Store backups under the project backups/ folder on D:.

Don’t

  • Don’t publish the app, this manual, or live books data.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, or recolour BL to a trade colour.
  • Don’t set body copy in gold or coral.
  • Don’t add a VAT column or claim VAT registration.
  • Don’t swap Inter/Segoe for a display serif in the product.
  • Don’t treat a mockup or wireframe PDF as this CI.
  • Don’t put keys or passwords in this file.
